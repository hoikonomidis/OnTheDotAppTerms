Be on time for every meeting

A small reminder in the corner is easy to miss. On the Dot covers your whole screen the minute a meeting starts, with one button to join. It is also your calendar, and it writes the meeting's transcript for you.

See how it works

Coming soon for Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 26.

  • Alerts you cannot miss
  • A full calendar built in
  • Transcripts and summaries
  • Works with Google Calendar
  • Your data stays on your Mac

Alerts

As loud or as quiet as you like

Fill the screen for the meetings that matter, and let the rest come quietly. Each calendar can have its own look.

  • Three sizes: the full screen, a band across the top, or a small card.
  • Alert at the start, or 1, 2 or 5 minutes before. Snooze for 1, 3 or 5 minutes.
  • Enter joins and Esc dismisses, so your hands stay on the keyboard.
  • While you share your screen, the alert stays small and is left out of the share.

Join

One click, and you're in

On the Dot finds the video link in the invitation, so the button always goes to the right place.

  • Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex and 28 more services.
  • Answer invitations with Yes, Maybe or No, right where you see them.
  • Today's meetings and a countdown to the next one live in the menu bar.

Calendar

Your week, without opening a browser

See your day, week or month, and change it in place. Your calendars keep their colours.

  • Add, move, change and delete events, with guests and a Meet link.
  • Type "Lunch with Anna tomorrow at 1" and the event is ready to save.
  • Shows the calendars set up on your Mac too, such as iCloud and Microsoft.

Leave by

Know when to go

For a meeting in a place, On the Dot can alert you when it's time to leave, from Apple Maps' travel time by car, transit or on foot. It is off until you turn it on.

Transcripts

Notes that write themselves, on your Mac

Start a transcript when you join. On the Dot writes down what was said, and can sum it up into key points and action items.

  • The sound is turned into text on your Mac. It never leaves it.
  • The recording is deleted as soon as the transcript is written.
  • Summaries are written on your Mac by Apple Intelligence, for meetings in English.

Your data stays on your Mac

The app talks to Google straight from your Mac. There is no On the Dot server, so nobody else ever sees your calendar, your meetings or your transcripts.

No server

Nothing you do in the app passes through anyone else's computer on its way to Google.

Encrypted

What the app keeps is encrypted on your Mac, and your sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, and no account to make. You sign in with Google and nothing else.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar, with one Google account or several. It can also show and alert for the calendars set up on your Mac, such as iCloud and Microsoft.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS 26.

Will other people see the alert when I share my screen?

No. While you share your screen, the alert shows as the band or the small card, and it is left out of the share.

Does it still alert me when I'm offline?

Yes. The app remembers the meetings it last read, so alerts still come when the network drops.

Do the other people know about a transcript?

Only if you tell them. In many places you must tell people, or ask them, before you record what they say, so please do.
