Never miss the start of a meeting

On the Dot is a Mac menu-bar app. The minute your next meeting starts, it covers every screen with an alert and a Join button. It is hard to miss on purpose.

The download is coming soon. It will need macOS 26 on a Mac with Apple silicon, and a Google account.

What it does

  • Full-screen alerts. When a meeting starts, or a few minutes before, an alert covers every screen and plays a sound until you join, snooze or dismiss it.
  • One-click Join. It finds the video link in the invitation: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams and many more.
  • Your calendar in the menu bar. See today's meetings, answer invitations, and add, move or delete events without opening a browser.
  • Leave by. For a meeting in a place, it can tell you when to go, from Apple Maps' travel time.
  • Transcripts written on your Mac. If you ask for one, the app writes the meeting's transcript on your Mac. The sound never leaves it.

What it asks Google for, and why

You sign in with Google in your browser. The app then asks for these permissions and nothing more.

PermissionWhat the app does with it
Your email, name and photoShows which account is signed in.
See your calendars
calendar.readonly, calendar.calendarlist		 Lists your calendars, so you choose which ones alert you, and reads their meetings.
See and change events
calendar.events		 Reads your meetings to alert you on time. Sends your answer to an invitation, and the events you add, move, change or delete in the app.
See your organisation's directory
directory.readonly		 Shows your colleagues' photos in a meeting's guest list, and suggests guests as you type. Only for Google Workspace accounts.

Your data stays on your Mac

The app talks to Google straight from your Mac. There is no On the Dot server, so the person who makes the app never sees your calendar, your meetings or your transcripts. What the app keeps is encrypted on your Mac, and your sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
