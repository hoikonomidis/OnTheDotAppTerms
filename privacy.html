Privacy policy

In effect from 14 November 2026.

The short version: your calendar and your transcripts stay on your Mac. I run no server, so I never receive them.

On the Dot ("the app") is made by one independent developer ("I"). There is no company behind it. This page says what the app reads, where it keeps it, and who else it talks to.

What the app reads from Google

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to read:

  • Your email address, name and photo, to show which account is signed in.
  • Your calendars and their events: titles, times, places, descriptions, guests, video links and attachments' names. The app uses them to alert you when a meeting starts and to show your calendar.
  • Your organisation's directory (Google Workspace accounts only): colleagues' names, email addresses and photos, to show photos in a guest list and suggest guests as you type.

The app also writes to your Google Calendar, only when you ask: your answer to an invitation, and the events you add, move, change or delete in the app.

Where it is kept

  • Everything the app reads from Google is kept on your Mac only, encrypted (AES-GCM) with a key in the macOS Keychain.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain. It lets the app read your calendar while you stay signed in.
  • The app's log holds times, counts and decisions. It never holds meeting titles, descriptions, places, links, names, email addresses or transcript text.

Who I share it with

Nobody. I do not have a server, so your Google data never reaches me. I do not sell it, show ads with it, give it to anyone, or use it to train AI models. No person reads it.

On the Dot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.

Calendars on your Mac

If you allow it, the app also reads the calendars set up in macOS (iCloud, Microsoft and others). They are read on your Mac and never sent anywhere.

Transcripts

  • A transcript is written only when you start one.
  • The sound of the meeting and your microphone is turned into text on your Mac. The sound never leaves it.
  • While the meeting goes on, the recording is encrypted with a key that exists only in memory. It is deleted as soon as the transcript is written.
  • The transcript's text is kept encrypted on your Mac for as long as you choose in Settings: 7, 30 or 90 days, or until you delete it.
  • If you ask for a summary, Apple Intelligence writes it on your Mac. The transcript is not sent anywhere, and the summary is kept encrypted with it.

If you write to me

If you send me an email, I use your address only to answer you. I do not pass it on, and I delete it if you ask.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out in the app. This revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the saved sign-in from your Mac.
  • To remove everything else the app keeps, quit it and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/MeetingReminder and the log folder ~/Library/Logs/MeetingReminder.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Google account, under Third-party apps and services.

Because your data is only on your Mac, I cannot see, export or delete it for you.

Children

The app is not made for children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle your data in a different way, I will change this page and its date before the change reaches you.
